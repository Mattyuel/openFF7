# World plan (for review)

Every field outside Midgar and Kalm that checks the story value, and the story moment it is
fixed at (generated by `tools/ff7tool.py plan-world` into `patches/world_moments.json`).
Change a field's moment by adding it to `patches/world_overrides.json`.

Rule: just after the field's last disc 1 story event; for fields with no events of their own, just
after their highest disc 1 check; fields whose checks are all disc 2+ get their disc 1 state (998).

New-game "seen" flags that silence entry scenes are in `patches/world_flags.json`
(`tools/ff7tool.py quiet-flags`). Many rooms listed here are only reachable during story scenes
(flashbacks, trains, the Highwind cutscenes); they are fixed anyway so nothing leaks.
